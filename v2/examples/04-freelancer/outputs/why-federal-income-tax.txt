  · standard deduction and taxed on the ordinary brackets. Long-term gains are
  · stacked on top of it and taxed on the capital-gains brackets. Tax already
  · withheld or paid is credited, and the rest is owed by April 15 of the next
  · year (Form 1040). A refund, when more was withheld than owed, is not booked.
  ·
  · The brackets depend on `filing`, so set `filing single` or `filing joint`
  · on the person who lives here.
