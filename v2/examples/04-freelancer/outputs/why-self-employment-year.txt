  · Net earnings are receipts less expenses. Self-employment tax is 12.4% Social

  · 20% qualified business income deduction is limited to 20% of taxable income
  · before it.
  ·
  · Self-employment tax is paid with the same estimated payments as income tax,
  · so what was paid first covers it, and only the rest is credited against
  · income tax by `us`.
