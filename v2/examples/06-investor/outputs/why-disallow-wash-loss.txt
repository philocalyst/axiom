Why disallow-wash-loss

  When      on in
  Governs   everything in this book
  Written   tax.ax:17
  Ran       1 times
  Recorded  0 violations, 2 effects
  · A wash sale (IRC §1091): a loss on a sale is disallowed when substantially
  · identical shares are bought within 30 days either side, and it is added to
  · the basis of the shares bought. Axiom cannot see the 30-day window, so the
  · loss is parked by hand in `equity/wash-sale` and released into the new lot's
  · cost when the replacement is bought. `us` counted the whole loss when the
  · shares were sold; this law adds the disallowed part back.
