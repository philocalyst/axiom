Why feie

  Law       feie
  ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  When      each year
  Governs   everyone living under us/abroad, and all they own
  Written   systems/us/abroad.ax:50
  Ran       1 time
  Recorded  0 violations, 4 effects
  · The foreign earned income exclusion (IRC §911): pay earned abroad, up to a
  · yearly limit prorated by the days spent abroad, is not taxed by the United
  · States. It comes off income as an adjustment. The income that stays is taxed
  · as if the excluded pay were on top of it (IRC §911(f), the "stacking rule"): the
  · extra tax is the tax on everything less the tax on the excluded pay less the
  · tax on what is left, and it is added to the year's other taxes.
  ·
  · To fix: `days-abroad` is the number of qualifying days in the year; the
  · exclusion cannot exceed the limit for those days or the pay earned abroad.
