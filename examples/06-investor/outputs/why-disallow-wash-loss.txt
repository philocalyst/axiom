  Recorded  0 violations, 1 effect
  · A wash sale (IRC §1091): a loss on a sale is disallowed when substantially
  · identical shares are bought within 30 days either side, and it is added to the
  · basis of the shares bought. The owner records the adjustment as a flow into the
  · basis of the new shares from a place of kind `wash-adjustment`. `us` counted the
  · whole loss when the shares were sold; this law adds back what the flow moved.
  ·
  · To fix: if a sale is not a wash sale, do not write the flow, and the loss stands.
